## WORK — 180 minutes

1. Assign facilitator/interviewer/note-taker/evidence controller/AI operator/observer.
2. Interview Primary User and Operational role in separate sessions.
3. Tag and log evidence (`CF/SN/CT/OP/AS/PS/OQ`).
4. Interview Policy/Manager and Technical/Privacy/Safety roles.
5. Select one conflict; identify positions/interests/authority/constraints/options.
6. Record negotiation status/rationale and create 5–8 evidence-linked RCs.
7. Present 4–5 minutes, update logs and submit.
